About Mi Tierra

A family-owned Mexican restaurant in Windsor.

Welcome to Mi Tierra, a family-owned Mexican restaurant in Windsor, Colorado, serving the bold, comforting flavors we love.

From hearty burritos and enchiladas to traditional favorites like pozole, menudo, lengua and carnitas, there’s something here for every craving. Whether you're stopping in for breakfast, grabbing lunch, or sitting down for dinner, we’re here to serve you a meal that feels like home.

Come hungry. Leave happy.

Family-Owned & Local

Proudly serving Windsor with the friendly, welcoming atmosphere of a neighborhood restaurant.

Fresh & Made With Care

Every meal is prepared with care, bringing you the comforting flavors and generous portions you’re looking for.

A Place to Come Back To

Whether it’s your first visit or your regular stop, Mi Tierra is a place to enjoy good food, good company, and a taste of home.
